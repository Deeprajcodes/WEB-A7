Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is one of the most successful and famous Indian cricketers of the modern era. Born on 5 November 1988 in Delhi, India, Kohli developed a passion for cricket from a very young age. He is a right-handed batsman who is known for his aggressive style, consistency, fitness and determination.

Kohli has represented India in international cricket and has achieved remarkable success in Test, One Day International and Twenty20 cricket. He became especially famous for his ability to chase difficult targets in ODI cricket and perform under pressure.

Apart from his batting, Virat Kohli is also known for his leadership and fitness. During his time as captain, he encouraged Indian players to become more athletic and competitive. His passion and intensity on the cricket field have made him an inspiration for millions of cricket fans.

Early Life and Cricket Journey

Beginning of his career

Virat Kohli started playing cricket in Delhi at a very young age. He joined the West Delhi Cricket Academy and worked hard to improve his batting skills. His talent quickly became noticeable and he progressed through Delhi's junior cricket system.

One of the biggest moments of his early career came in 2008 when Kohli captained the Indian Under-19 team to victory in the ICC Under-19 World Cup in Malaysia. His leadership and batting performances helped establish him as one of India's most promising young cricketers.

After his Under-19 success, Kohli continued performing well in domestic cricket. His consistent performances eventually earned him a place in the Indian senior team.

International Career

Rise of a young Indian batsman

Virat Kohli made his ODI debut for India in 2008 against Sri Lanka. During the early part of his international career, he gradually developed his batting technique and became an important member of the Indian team.

In the 2011 Cricket World Cup, Kohli became the first Indian batsman to score a century on his World Cup debut. India went on to win the tournament by defeating Sri Lanka in the final.

Kohli's ability to score runs while chasing targets soon became one of his biggest strengths. He became known for calculating the required run rate and controlling an innings according to the situation of the match.

Captaincy and Leadership

Virat Kohli as captain

Virat Kohli became India's Test captain and later led India in limited-overs cricket. Under his leadership, the Indian team developed a strong fast-bowling attack and placed a greater emphasis on fitness.

One of the most important achievements of Kohli's Test captaincy was India's historic Test series victory in Australia during the 2018–19 tour. India became the first Asian team to win a Test series in Australia.

Kohli became India's most successful Test captain by the number of Test victories. His leadership was known for its aggressive approach and strong desire to win matches.

Record-Breaking Batting

One of the greatest ODI batsmen

Virat Kohli has created numerous records during his international career. He became one of the fastest players to reach major run milestones in ODI cricket.

In November 2023, during the Cricket World Cup semi-final against New Zealand, Kohli scored his 50th ODI century. He became the first batsman to reach 50 centuries in ODI cricket.

Kohli had an outstanding 2023 Cricket World Cup. He scored 765 runs during the tournament and was named the Player of the Tournament.

2024 T20 World Cup

A memorable World Cup victory

The 2024 T20 World Cup became another important chapter in Virat Kohli's career. After a difficult tournament in the group stage, Kohli produced his best performance in the final against South Africa.

In the final, Kohli scored 76 runs from 59 balls. His innings helped India post a competitive total of 176 runs.

India eventually defeated South Africa by seven runs and won the T20 World Cup. Kohli was named Player of the Match for his important innings.

After India's victory, Kohli announced his retirement from T20 International cricket. He finished his T20I career as one of the most successful batsmen in the history of the format.

Test Career and Retirement

A remarkable Test career

Test cricket played an important role in Virat Kohli's career. He made his Test debut against the West Indies in 2011 and eventually became one of India's most important Test batsmen.

As Test captain, Kohli helped develop a strong Indian bowling attack and encouraged a culture of fitness and aggressive cricket.

In May 2025, Virat Kohli announced his retirement from Test cricket. He finished his Test career with more than 9,000 runs and 30 Test centuries.

Achievements and Awards

Major achievements

Virat Kohli has received several major awards during his career. He received the Arjuna Award in 2013, the Padma Shri in 2017 and the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna Award in 2018.

He has also received several ICC awards for his performances in international cricket and was recognised as one of the leading players of his generation.

Some of his biggest achievements include winning the 2011 ODI World Cup, the 2013 Champions Trophy and the 2024 T20 World Cup.

Legacy

Virat Kohli's legacy is not limited to the number of runs and centuries he has scored. He has also influenced a generation of young cricketers through his focus on fitness, discipline and preparation.

His aggressive approach, confidence and determination have made him one of the most recognisable personalities in world cricket.

From a young boy playing cricket in Delhi to becoming one of the biggest names in international cricket, Virat Kohli's journey represents the importance of talent, hard work, discipline and determination.
